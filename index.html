Measured result

Measured on 2026-10-15 against a hand labeled set of 20 documents, with 133 gold entities and 66 gold relations: entities F1 0.904 and relations F1 0.598.

The misses are named, not hidden: the tool misses names its word lists do not know, such as Warsaw and Neil Armstrong, and it cannot follow pronouns across sentences.

Full scores, misses, and how they were measured: The proof in the documentation.

About

You give Graph a piece of text. It finds the people, groups, places, dates, and tools the text names. It also finds links the text states, such as who founded a group, where a group is based, and who works for it. It puts the names and links in a map you can search and check.

It works by fixed rules and word lists. It does not use a large language model. It was built to learn how far plain rules can go on their own.
