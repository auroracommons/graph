# Collaborate on Graph

Graph serves one problem family: information
chaos and unorganized knowledge. This page lists concrete, scoped
ways an organization can work with the project. Every task here is
real and completable. Nothing on this page implies an existing
partnership; collaborations are invitations until they actually
happen, and no partner is named publicly without their written
agreement.

## Who this is for

Civic agencies, newsrooms, research institutes, and data holders.

## Sized tasks

1. **Run the explorer on a public dataset and publish the eval.**
   Take a document set you already work with (meeting minutes,
   archives, public filings), label a sample by hand with the rules
   in `eval/README.md`, and publish the precision and recall next to
   the built-in set's scores. Hard domains are welcome; a dataset
   where the extractor does badly teaches more than one where it
   shines.
2. **Contribute a labeled corpus.** Extend `eval/eval_set.json`
   with documents from your field, labeled under the same rules.
   Every added document makes the published numbers mean more, and
   labeling disagreements are recorded, not smoothed over.
3. **Propose query patterns for a real investigation.** Describe a
   question your team actually asks of its documents. We will say
   plainly whether the query engine can express it, extend it if
   the gap is small, and document the answer either way.

## What you get

A public, dated record in this repository: the dataset eval, the
labeled corpus addition, or the query pattern write-up, with your
organization's role stated as you approve it.

## Start

Open an issue in this repository describing the task you have in
mind and the documents it involves. Small and specific beats grand
and vague.
